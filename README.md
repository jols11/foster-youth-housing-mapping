# Mapping Housing Preferences and Resources for Foster Youth

## Overview

This project uses geospatial analysis to explore housing needs and resources for former foster youth. Using QGIS, I mapped geographic patterns in housing preferences and available resources to better understand how well existing resources align with the needs of young people transitioning out of foster care.

## Research Question

**How do the geographic distribution of housing resources and the housing preferences of former foster youth compare?**

## Methodology

I used QGIS to:

* Organize and visualize geographic data related to foster youth housing
* Map the locations and distribution of relevant housing resources
* Visualize reported housing preferences across geographic areas
* Compare patterns in resource availability and housing needs
* Create maps that make geographic disparities and relationships easier to identify

## Tools

* **QGIS** — geospatial analysis and visualization
* **Geospatial data** — mapping and spatial comparison
* **Data visualization** — communicating geographic patterns and disparities

## Motivation

Access to stable housing is an important factor in successful transitions out of foster care. I was interested in using spatial analysis to move beyond aggregate statistics and examine **where** resources are located and how their distribution relates to the needs and preferences of foster youth.

## Takeaways

The maps provide a geographic perspective on foster youth housing that can complement traditional quantitative analysis. By visualizing both resources and preferences spatially, the project helps identify areas where existing resources may align with—or diverge from—identified housing needs.

## About

This project was developed in the summer of 2025 as a Data Analytics & Policy intern at Safe Plcae for Youth. As part of my research on educational and housing challenges facing foster youth, the goal was to use data visualization and geospatial analysis to make patterns in the data more accessible and actionable.
